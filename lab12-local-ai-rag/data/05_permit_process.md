# Elmwood Permit and Review Process

*Fictional document for teaching. Elmwood is not a real city.*
Updated January 2025. Status: adopted.

## 1. Types of review

- **By-right (administrative) review**: staff check the application against the code. No public hearing. Typical time: 30 days. Examples: single-family homes, ADUs, duplexes in R-2, rooftop solar.
- **Conditional use review**: requires a public hearing before the Planning Commission. Typical time: 90 days.
- **Variance**: permission to depart from a dimensional rule (height, setback) because of a hardship unique to the property. Decided by the Zoning Board of Appeals after a public hearing.

## 2. Short-term rental permit

- Apply online through the city permit portal.
- Fee: $150 per year.
- Required documents: proof of primary residence (for example a driver's license or voter registration at the address), proof of liability insurance, and a floor plan showing exits and smoke detectors.
- The permit must be renewed every year.
- A permit may be revoked after three verified complaints within 12 months.

## 3. Building permit fees

- ADU: $500 flat fee.
- New single-family home: $1,200 plus $0.25 per square foot.
- Rooftop solar: no fee.

## 4. Public notice

For conditional uses and variances, the city mails notice to every property owner within 300 feet at least 15 days before the hearing, and posts a sign on the property.

## 5. Appeals

A decision by staff may be appealed to the Zoning Board of Appeals within 30 days. The appeal fee is $250. A decision by the Zoning Board of Appeals may be appealed to the county court.
