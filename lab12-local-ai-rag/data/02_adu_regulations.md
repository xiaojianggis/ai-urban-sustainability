# Elmwood Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Regulations

*Fictional document for teaching. Elmwood is not a real city.*
Adopted June 2024. Status: adopted.

## Section 1. What is an ADU?

An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is a small, self-contained home on the same lot as a main house. It has its own kitchen, bathroom, and entrance. ADUs are also called granny flats, in-law suites, carriage houses, or backyard cottages.

ADUs may be:

- **Attached**: part of, or added onto, the main house (for example a basement or attic unit).
- **Detached**: a separate building in the yard, or built above a detached garage.

## Section 2. Where ADUs are allowed

One ADU is permitted per lot in the R-1, R-2 and R-3 districts. An ADU is permitted by right: no public hearing is required.

## Section 3. Size limits

- A detached ADU may not exceed 800 square feet or 50 percent of the floor area of the main house, whichever is smaller.
- An attached ADU may not exceed 50 percent of the floor area of the main house.
- Every ADU must be at least 250 square feet.

## Section 4. Height and setbacks

- Maximum height of a detached ADU: 20 feet, or 25 feet if built above a garage.
- Minimum side and rear setback for a detached ADU: 5 feet.
- A detached ADU must be at least 10 feet from the main house.

## Section 5. Parking

No additional off-street parking space is required for an ADU.

## Section 6. Occupancy

- The owner of the lot is not required to live on the property.
- An ADU may not be used as a short-term rental (fewer than 30 nights).
- An ADU may not be sold separately from the main house.
