# Elmwood Parking and Transportation Standards

*Fictional document for teaching. Elmwood is not a real city.*
Adopted March 2024. Status: adopted.

## Section 1. Purpose

These standards reduce the amount of land used for parking, lower housing costs, and support walking, cycling and transit, consistent with the climate goals of the Elmwood 2045 Comprehensive Plan.

## Section 2. Minimum parking requirements

Outside the areas listed in Section 3, the following minimums apply:

- Single-family home: 1 space per dwelling.
- Multifamily housing: 1 space per dwelling unit.
- Retail and offices: 2 spaces per 1,000 square feet of floor area.
- Accessory dwelling units: no space required.

## Section 3. Reduced parking near transit

- **No minimums near rail.** Within one-quarter mile of a rail station there is no minimum parking requirement for any use.
- **Maximums in MU-T.** In the MU-T Mixed-Use Transit district, parking is capped at a maximum of 0.75 spaces per dwelling unit and 1.5 spaces per 1,000 square feet of commercial floor area.
- **Frequent bus corridors.** Within one-quarter mile of a bus route with service every 15 minutes or better, minimum parking requirements are reduced by 50 percent.

## Section 4. Bicycle parking

- Multifamily housing: 1 secure long-term bicycle space per dwelling unit.
- Retail and offices: 1 short-term bicycle space per 2,000 square feet of floor area.

## Section 5. Electric vehicle charging

In any new parking lot with 10 or more spaces, at least 20 percent of spaces must have an electric vehicle charger, and all remaining spaces must be wired so a charger can be added later.

## Section 6. Unbundled parking

In new multifamily buildings with 20 or more units, parking must be rented or sold separately from the housing unit, so that residents without cars do not pay for parking.
