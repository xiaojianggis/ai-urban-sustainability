# Elmwood 2045 Comprehensive Plan — Chapter 6: Climate and Environment

*Fictional document for teaching. Elmwood is not a real city.*
Adopted November 2023. Status: adopted.

## 6.1 Vision

By 2045, Elmwood will be a cooler, greener, low-carbon city where every resident can walk to shade, transit and a park within ten minutes.

## 6.2 Greenhouse gas emissions

- Goal: reduce citywide greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions 50 percent below 2005 levels by 2030.
- Goal: reach net-zero emissions by 2045.
- The largest sources today are buildings (44 percent) and transportation (38 percent).

## 6.3 Urban tree canopy and heat

- Elmwood's tree canopy covers 24 percent of the city today.
- Goal: increase tree canopy to 35 percent by 2045.
- Priority: plant first in the neighborhoods with the least canopy and the highest summer surface temperatures. The Eastside and Mill District neighborhoods have less than 12 percent canopy.
- Every new street project must include street trees where the sidewalk is at least 8 feet wide.

## 6.4 Buildings and energy

- All new city buildings will be all-electric from 2026.
- The city will offer free energy audits to owners of homes built before 1960.
- Rooftop solar is permitted by right in every zoning district.

## 6.5 Transportation

- Goal: 40 percent of trips by walking, cycling or transit by 2035.
- Allow more homes near the rail stations (see the MU-T district) and reduce parking requirements near transit.

## 6.6 Stormwater and flooding

- New development on lots over one acre must manage the first 1.5 inches of rainfall on site.
- The city will map flood risk for the Elm Creek watershed and update it every five years.

## 6.7 Equity

Climate investments should benefit the residents most exposed to heat, flooding and air pollution first. Programs must be offered in English, Spanish and Vietnamese, the three most common languages spoken at home in Elmwood.
