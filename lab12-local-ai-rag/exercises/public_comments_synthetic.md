# Public Comments on the Draft Riverside Station Area Plan

*Synthetic comments written for teaching. All names are invented; no real person is described.*
Comment period: September 2026. 24 comments received.

**Comment 1 — Resident, Riverside.** I support more homes near the station. My kids cannot afford to live in the town they grew up in.

**Comment 2 — Resident, Riverside.** Please keep the 75-foot height limit away from Oak Street. The new buildings would shade our gardens all afternoon.

**Comment 3 — Business owner, Main Street.** More residents within walking distance would help the shops on Main Street. I support the plan.

**Comment 4 — Resident, Mill District.** I worry about parking. If new buildings have fewer spaces, people will park on our street.

**Comment 5 — Resident, Riverside.** The plan is good. Add more trees on the station plaza; it is very hot in summer.

**Comment 6 — Cyclist, Eastside.** Protected bike lanes to the station should be built before the new housing, not after.

**Comment 7 — Resident, Riverside.** I support the plan, especially the affordable housing bonus.

**Comment 8 — Resident, Oak Street.** Traffic on Oak Street is already bad at rush hour. How will the city handle hundreds of new cars?

**Comment 9 — Senior resident, Riverside.** Please add benches and shade along the walk to the station. Many of us cannot walk far in the heat.

**Comment 10 — Resident, Mill District.** Generally supportive. The sidewalks near the station need repair first.

**Comment 11 — Developer.** The 15 percent affordability requirement for the bonus is reasonable. Please speed up permit review.

**Comment 12 — Tenant, Mill District.** I rent an older apartment two blocks from the station. When the new buildings come, my landlord will raise the rent or sell. Families like mine will be pushed out. What will the city do to protect current tenants?

**Comment 13 — Resident, Riverside.** I support the plan. Riverside needs a grocery store near the station.

**Comment 14 — Resident, Oak Street.** Too tall, too dense. This will change the character of the neighborhood.

**Comment 15 — Parent, Riverside.** Will the elementary school have room for more children?

**Comment 16 — Resident, Eastside (comment submitted in Spanish, translated by staff).** The meetings about this plan were only in English and the notices were only in English. Many of my neighbors did not know about the plan or how to comment. Please hold a meeting in Spanish and in Vietnamese.

**Comment 17 — Resident, Riverside.** Strongly support. Less parking is fine; I sold my car last year.

**Comment 18 — Resident, Riverside.** Support, but please require green roofs or solar on the new buildings.

**Comment 19 — Small business owner, Mill District.** I support more housing, but construction will block my storefront for months. Is there help for small businesses during construction?

**Comment 20 — Resident, Oak Street.** Please study parking before approving the plan.

**Comment 21 — Resident, Elm Creek area.** The station area floods after heavy rain. The plan adds a lot of pavement but says almost nothing about stormwater. Who is responsible when basements flood?

**Comment 22 — Resident, Riverside.** I am in favor. More neighbors, more life on the street.

**Comment 23 — Resident, Mill District.** Generally supportive of the plan and the bike lanes.

**Comment 24 — Resident, Riverside.** Support. Please make sure the new park on the plaza is open to everyone, not just the new residents.
